**Effective:** [EFFECTIVE DATE]

This policy forms part of the [Terms of Service](terms-of-service.md). It
applies to you and to anyone using the platform through your account, including
your users.

---

## 1. Do not use Bifrost to break the law

No unlawful activity, and nothing that facilitates it — including fraud, money
laundering, sanctions evasion, or trading in stolen credentials or data.

## 2. Do not attack the platform

Specifically, do not:

- Access, or try to access, another customer's data, tenant or database
- Probe, scan or test the security of the platform except as permitted under the
  responsible-disclosure terms in clause 8 of the Terms of Service
- Circumvent authentication, rate limits, quotas or access controls
- Interfere with service to others — denial of service, resource exhaustion,
  deliberate flooding
- Reverse engineer or decompile the platform, except where law grants that right
  regardless of contract

## 3. Do not abuse the messaging channels

Bifrost sends email, SMS and Telegram messages on your behalf. Do not use them
for unsolicited bulk messaging, phishing, or anything designed to deceive a
recipient about who is contacting them.

Authentication messages must be authentication messages. Do not smuggle
marketing into a one-time code.

Only trigger a message to someone who asked to receive it. SMS in particular
costs real money and abuse is grounds for immediate suspension.

## 4. Do not misrepresent identity

Do not configure an application to impersonate another business, imply an
endorsement you do not have, or present a sign-in screen designed to look like
someone else's.

Your application's name, logo and branding must be yours to use.

## 5. Payments must be genuine

Do not use payment flows to process transactions unrelated to your own stated
business, to test with stolen card or account details, or to structure
transactions to evade a provider's limits or reporting.

You must comply with your payment provider's own rules. Their suspension of you
is not our failure.

## 6. Content limits

Do not store or transmit through the platform:

- Malware, or anything designed to damage or gain unauthorised access
- Child sexual abuse material — reported to the authorities without notice to you
- Content that incites violence or unlawful discrimination
- Material infringing someone else's intellectual property

## 7. Data you must not route through the platform

Bifrost is not built for, and must not receive:

- Payment card numbers, CVVs or magnetic stripe data. Card data belongs with your
  payment provider. The platform is not PCI-DSS certified.
- Government identity numbers, passport or national ID numbers
- Health, biometric or genetic data
- Any other special category of personal data, without a written agreement first

If you need to handle these, talk to us before you start rather than after.

## 8. Resource use

Use the platform in proportion to your plan. Do not resell access, share
credentials across unrelated businesses, or use one registered application as a
front for many unrelated products.

Automated access must respect published rate limits and identify itself
honestly.

## 9. Reporting abuse

Report suspected abuse to [SECURITY CONTACT EMAIL] with enough detail to
investigate.

## 10. Enforcement

Breaches may lead to a warning, throttling, suspension of a feature or account,
or termination — proportionate to severity, whether it was deliberate, and
whether it is repeated.

We will normally give notice and a chance to fix the problem first. **We will
not, and will act immediately, where there is a live risk to the platform, to
another customer, or to someone's safety, or where the law requires it.**

## 11. Changes

We may update this policy; material changes take effect [NOTICE PERIOD] days
after notice.
